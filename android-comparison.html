iSync365 vs. CiraSync — 3-Year Android Cost Comparison

Android isn't where we focus first, but it's worth showing the real numbers for teams weighing the two platforms. Figures as of 2026.

The scenario

A 20-user team, all on Outlook and Android phones. On Android, iSync365 works through a third-party CardDAV app called DAVx⁵ — a one-time purchase, not a subscription. CiraSync's Android delivery uses its own free companion app, CardDAV Direct. Everything below assumes the business covers the DAVx⁵ purchase for all 20 users — the more conservative case for iSync365. If employees cover their own $6-ish one-time app purchase instead (common for personal devices), the gap in the company's own costs is even larger than shown here.

  iSync365 CiraSync
Monthly rate $2.50/user/month $4.00/user/month
Annual subscription (20 users) $600 $960
Android companion app DAVx⁵ — one-time purchase, roughly $6.49/user (~$130 for 20 users) Free
Year 1 total $729.80 $960.00
2-year total $1,329.80 $1,920.00
3-year total $1,929.80 $2,880.00

The bottom line

Even absorbing the entire DAVx⁵ cost on the business's side, iSync365 comes out ahead in year one — and the gap keeps growing every year after that, since the app is a one-time cost while the subscription gap compounds annually. Over 3 years, that's roughly $950 saved for a 20-user team.

Pricing shown reflects publicly listed rates as of 2026. App store pricing can change over time — check current listings before relying on exact figures.
