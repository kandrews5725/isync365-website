There are a few solid tools in this space already. As a contact distribution platform — built to organize and deliver contact lists to Outlook and iPhone, not to sync everything under the sun — here's an honest look at where iSync365 fits, so you can decide what's right for your organization.

CiraSync

Established Outlook/mobile sync tool with several delivery methods to choose from. Full multi-list management sits behind its Enterprise plan; delivering a truly separate address book to unmanaged devices requires MDM or their companion app.

Contactzilla

Clean, mobile-first CardDAV sync. No Outlook/M365 mailbox sync.

SyncPenguin

General-purpose data sync (calendars, CRMs, e-commerce). Not purpose-built for directory distribution.

iSync365

Outlook and mobile together, dedicated CardDAV account per device — no MDM required — organized by list, access updates automatically.

What about calendar sync?

Some tools in this space bundle shared-calendar sync as part of their pitch. Outlook mobile now natively supports toggling visibility for shared and resource calendars — conference rooms, team schedules, and the like — right in the app. That's largely closed the gap calendar-sync add-ons were built to solve. iSync365 stays focused on the problem Outlook and your phone still can't solve on their own: getting your company's contact directory onto every device, organized the way your business actually works.

Every option here can get you part of the way there.

If you want one tool that does the whole job — Outlook and mobile, organized by list, and works on personal phones without enrolling them in company device management — that's exactly what we built.
