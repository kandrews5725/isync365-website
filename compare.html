See how iSync365 delivers more — for less — than the alternatives.

There are a few solid tools in this space already. iSync365 is a contact distribution platform — built to organize and deliver contact lists to Outlook and iPhone, not to sync everything under the sun. Here's an honest look at where it fits, so you can decide what's right for your organization.

iSync365

Outlook and mobile together, dedicated CardDAV account per device — no MDM required — organized by list, access updates automatically.

CiraSync

An enterprise-grade sync tool built for larger IT teams — multi-list management requires its Enterprise plan, and separate address books on unmanaged devices need MDM or a companion app.

Contactzilla

Clean, mobile-first CardDAV sync, priced per device. Outlook/M365 sync isn't part of the base plan — it's a separate paid add-on.

SyncPenguin

General-purpose data sync (calendars, CRMs, e-commerce). Not purpose-built for directory distribution.
