Features

Everything's included — no add-ons, no higher tier required.

Every capability below ships in the base product. Nothing is locked behind an upgrade.

✓

Admin-controlled list membership

You decide who gets which contact list — by role, department, office, project, etc., or however your business is organized. Nothing is company-wide by default. Adding or removing someone from a list takes effect automatically, everywhere that list is delivered.

✓

Lists built from your directory, or built by hand

Pull lists directly from your existing Microsoft 365/Entra ID groups — so as people join or leave a group, the list updates itself. Or build and maintain a list manually for contacts that live outside your directory entirely: vendors, subcontractors, clients, board members. Mix both approaches across different lists as needed.

✓

Separate, named address books on every phone

Each list shows up on mobile as its own clearly labeled address book — never merged into one giant contacts dump, and never mixed with someone's personal contacts. "Vendors" stays "Vendors." Removing a list removes exactly that address book, nothing else.

✓

Choose exactly where each list goes

Assign a contact list to Outlook, to mobile, or both — independently, per person. Someone might need a vendor list on their phone in the field but not cluttering their Outlook, while an office admin gets the same list in both places. That kind of split isn't possible with tools that rely on Exchange ActiveSync for mobile delivery, since EAS only ever mirrors whatever's already sitting in the Outlook mailbox.

✓

No company phone? No MDM required.

iSync365 works on personal, unmanaged devices. There's no mobile device management enrollment, no company profile taking over someone's phone — just a simple, one-time account setup that takes a couple of minutes.

✓

Contacts appear in Outlook automatically

No app to install, no button for employees to press. Once a list is assigned to someone, it's written directly into their Outlook contacts — already there the next time they open it, on desktop or mobile.

✓

Bring in contacts from anywhere

Pull lists from your Microsoft 365/Entra directory, or build one manually — add contacts one at a time, or import a CSV in bulk. Not every contact you need to distribute lives in a directory or a CRM; iSync365 doesn't require one to exist somewhere else first.

✓

Contact photos included

Add a photo to any contact, and it's pushed right along with everything else — so the people your team calls most show up recognizable on the phone, not just a name.

✓

Self-service, isolated by company

Each organization gets its own dedicated space — your directory, your lists, your users, fully separate from every other company using iSync365. Nothing is shared or visible across organizations.

✓

Clean offboarding, by design

When someone is removed from a group, or unassigned from a list, their access is revoked, automatically, on every device. There's no separate cleanup step and nothing left behind to track down later.
