Features

Everything's included — no add-ons, no higher tier required.

Everything below ships in the base product — nothing locked behind an upgrade.

A real list, with a group assigned as its recipient — synced to both Outlook and mobile.

✓

Admin-controlled list membership

You decide who gets which contact list — by role, department, office, project, etc. Nothing is company-wide by default. New assignments sync automatically each night on both Outlook and mobile devices. Removals happen immediately instead — the moment someone's unassigned or a list is deleted, no manual upkeep required.

✓

Lists built from your directory, or built by hand

Create lists directly from your existing Microsoft 365 groups — so as membership changes, the lists update themselves. Or build lists manually for external contacts: vendors, subcontractors, clients, board members. Already tracking contacts in a spreadsheet? Import a CSV directly, no need to start over. Mix both approaches across different lists as needed.

✓

Separate, organized lists — wherever they land

Each list shows up in Outlook and on mobile devices as its own clearly labeled category or list — never merged into one giant contacts dump, and never mixed with someone's personal contacts. Assign a list to Outlook, to mobile, or both — independently, per person.

✓

Zero-touch on managed devices, simple setup without MDM

Managed devices get automatic, zero-touch delivery — no app, no button, nothing for the employee to do. Personal, unmanaged devices need a quick one-time manual setup instead, entered directly in the phone's own Settings app — no MDM enrollment, no third-party app required.

✓

Contact photos included

Add a photo to any contact, and it's pushed right along with everything else — so the people your team calls most show up recognizable on the phone, not just a name.

✓

Self-service, isolated by company

Each organization gets its own dedicated space — your directory, your lists, your users, fully separate from every other company using iSync365. Nothing is shared or visible across organizations.

Already use the Global Address List (GAL) and Outlook's Save Contacts option?

Having a GAL doesn't mean those contacts show up anywhere useful — entries are searchable if you already know who you're looking for, but not browsable, and they don't sync to your phone at all. Save Contacts only pushes what's already in your personal Outlook contacts, not the GAL — getting a whole team onto a phone this way means adding people one at a time. iSync365 pushes real, organized, scrollable lists into both Outlook and your phone automatically, for everyone.
